Who We Are

Not your average SMP.

Ender SMP started as a small crew and a cracked server jar, and grew into a community that treats the End dimension as prime real estate instead of a boss-fight footnote. Chorus fruit farms, floating obsidian bases, shulker economies — if it glows purple, we've probably built a city out of it.

No pay-to-win, no corporate spawn-shop energy. Just survival multiplayer done properly, with custom plugins written by people who actually play the server.

Getting In

Four steps. No essays required.

Join our Discord

This is where the actual connect IP lives — we rotate hosts occasionally, so the website won't always be current, but Discord always is.

Grab the IP

Head to #server-info in Discord and copy the current address. Takes about four seconds.

Add the server

Java Edition, version 1.21.x. Multiplayer → Add Server → paste the IP. No port number needed.

Fall through the portal

You'll spawn on the hub island. Grab a starter kit, punch some end stone, and go find your corner of the void.

Ready to fall in?

Join the Discord, grab the IP, and we'll see you on the other side of the portal.
