Three ideas, one world

Ender SMP is a private-feeling survival server built around a simple pitch. Play the game you know, in a world big enough for everyone's projects.

Semi-vanilla

The game you know, with the rough edges lightly sanded. Survival works like survival, so every farm, build and diamond still has to be earned.

Hermitcraft-style

One big shared world with big personal projects. Build a base with a point of view, join a collab, open a shop and watch the map fill up.

Community first

Small enough that people know each other, organised enough that nobody gets griefed. The rules are short and the staff are easy to reach.

Where we sit on the spectrum

Pure vanilla is the game with nothing added. Heavy modpacks change nearly everything. Ender SMP lives closer to vanilla, because we like Minecraft the way it ships, plus a few light quality-of-life tweaks.
