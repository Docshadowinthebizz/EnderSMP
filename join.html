How to join

Four steps, no riddles, no ender dragon required. Most people are in the world within a few minutes.

  1. Join our Discord

    Everything starts here. It is where the community lives and where the server details are shared.

    Open the invite

  2. Read the rules

    It takes about two minutes and saves everyone a lot of awkward conversations later.

    Read the rules

  3. Get the server details

    Once you are in the Discord, the server address and version are shared there. If anything is confusing, ask in the Discord and someone will point you the right way.

  4. Say hi and start building

    Introduce yourself, pick a spot, and ask your neighbours before building close. Then make something that looks great from far away.

Quick questions

The things new players ask most. Tap a question to open it.

What does semi-vanilla mean?

It plays like normal survival with a few light quality-of-life tweaks and no shortcuts. The Features page has the full explanation.

Where do I find the server address?

In our Discord. We keep it there so it is always up to date, and so we can say hello when you arrive.

Can I build anywhere?

Mostly, yes. Explore, pick a spot you like, and ask nearby players before building close to them. See the rules for the polite details.

Someone broke a rule. What do I do?

Tell staff in the Discord with screenshots or coordinates. Please do not take revenge, it only makes the paperwork longer.

Can I bring friends?

Friends are welcome. Send them the Discord invite and they can follow the same steps as you.

Step one is the easy one. Hop in.
